# First-shift criterion (not claimed new)

For prime p=1 mod 24, x0=(p+3)/4 and M=p*x0 are both 1 mod 3. At t=0, A=3, so a valid divisor d of M squared must be 2 mod 3. This occurs exactly when x0 has a prime factor q=2 mod 3. Choose d=q and e=M squared divided by q; both are 2 mod 3 and give positive integer denominators. Exactly 47,137 of 82,887 scanned primes passed this criterion.
