# Erdős–Straus research attempt

UTC: 2026-10-14T12:11:52Z
Status: REPRODUCED / VERIFIED (finite computation only)

Investigated primes p = 1 (mod 24) to 10 million. Exact divisor search found representations for all 82,887 primes, with maximal least first-denominator shift 26, attained at p=8,803,369. This is not a proof of the open conjecture.

Source: https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/aa42/aa42.pdf

Exact witness: 4/8803369 = 1/2200869 + 1/181085300330 + 1/3293760527702370.

Method: x=(p+3)/4+t, A=4x-p, M=px. The equation is equivalent to (Ay-M)(Az-M)=M². Enumerate d|M² with d+M and M²/d+M both divisible by A; then y=(d+M)/A and z=(M²/d+M)/A. Factoring x suffices because gcd(p,x)=1 in the scanned range.
