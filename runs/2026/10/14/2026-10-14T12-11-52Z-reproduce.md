# Reproduction recipe

Python 3.13.5, SymPy 1.14.0. Iterate `p in primerange(5,10000001)` with `p%24==1`. For t=0..30 set `x=(p+3)//4+t`, `A=4*x-p`, `M=p*x`. Factor x; enumerate divisors of x squared, multiplied by 1,p,p squared. Find d dividing M squared with `(d+M)%A==0` and `(M*M//d+M)%A==0`. Stop at the first successful t, count t, and verify `4/p=1/x+1/y+1/z` using exact rational arithmetic. A nonempty candidate is exhaustive for that fixed x.
